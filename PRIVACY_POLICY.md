# Privacy Policy — SuperPower PiP — Smart Picture-in-Picture

**Last updated:** November 5, 2026

The "SuperPower PiP — Smart Picture-in-Picture" extension (hereinafter "the Extension") is designed to provide an optimized Picture-in-Picture viewing experience. Respect for your privacy is our absolute priority.

## 1. Data collection and processing

We firmly believe that your data belongs to you.

- **No personal data:** The Extension does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal, identifiable, or sensitive data.
- **No activity tracking:** We do not track your browsing history, viewing habits, or the websites you visit.
- **100% local processing:** All analysis scripts (video detection and scoring) run locally and exclusively in your browser. No data is sent to any external server.

## 2. Use of permissions

The Extension requests strict and minimal permissions, required for its proper functioning:

- `activeTab`: Allows temporary access to the active tab only when you click the extension icon to search for a video.
- `scripting`: Required to temporarily inject the video capture script into the active page upon your request.
- `storage`: Used exclusively to locally save your display preferences (theme choice, advanced mode).
- `contextMenus`: Allows adding a shortcut in your browser’s right-click menu.
- `tabs`: Allows reading the title and URL of the active tab so the popup can display it. No browsing history is accessed.

## 3. Third-party services

The Extension does not integrate any third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or trackers.

## 4. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated to reflect potential technical changes. However, our philosophy will remain the same: zero data collection.
